Why Global Trackers Fail Indian Households

  • Cost Barrier: AngelSense, Medical Guardian & SmartSole cost ₹1.0L–₹1.39L over 2 years (exceeding annual rural care cost of ₹66,025).
  • Daily Charging Trap: 16–24 hr batteries die before an elopement occurs.
  • Indian RCC Indoor Blindness: Concrete flats block GPS, triggering phantom geofence alarms and rapid drain.
  • Telecom Mismatch: No nationwide Indian LTE-M; Jio/Airtel/Vi lock smartwatch eSIMs to ₹399+/mo postpaid plans.

Why Generic Consumer Gadgets Are Unsafe

  • Apple AirTags: Zero live GPS outside iPhone crowds and emit non-disableable anti-stalking beeps after 8–24 h, frightening wearers.
  • Shoe Insoles: Fail in Indian homes where people walk barefoot indoors and remove shoes at temples/homes.
  • Touchscreen Watches: 60.6% of PwDs struggle to operate tracking devices; 25–33% refuse or abandon bulky trackers.
  • Wrist-Only Fall Alerts: Miss slow, sliding geriatric collapses while false-alarming when dropped.

02 • Architecture & Differentiators

4-Pillar Ecosystem & Four Non-Commodity Innovations

A. SafeCircle Band

Screenless matte-graphite 4G LTE Cat-1 bis + multi-GNSS wrist capsule with recessed 3-s SOS, optical wear sensor & NFC/QR.

B. Home Beacon

₹439 plug-in ESP32-C3 BLE anchor that detects indoor presence, halts futile GPS hunting, and catches door exits in <15 s.

C. ROBOKRITI Cloud

AWS IoT Core Mumbai (MQTTS) + Firebase Firestore + Cloud Functions tiered escalation + Indian DLT SMS dead-zone fallback.

D. Care Mobile App

Elderly-accessible multilingual Flutter app with Honest Maps error circles, Care Circle claim-lock & Guided Dial-112 card.

Innovation 1 & 2: Power + Zero-Power Safety

  • BLE Home Beacon Power-Gating & Exit-Latch: Keeps GNSS off indoors (saving 44.4% to 50.3% daily energy) while IMU + beacon loss fires sub-15 s Nighttime Exit Watch alerts.
  • Zero-Power NFC + Privacy-Gated QR ID: Leverages India's universal UPI QR fluency; stays readable at 0% battery & 0 signal, hiding home address until caregiver activates Lost Mode.

Innovation 3 & 4: Geriatric Accuracy + India OPEX

  • Optical-Skin-Gated Edge-AI Fall & Long-Lie Pipeline: Fuses LSM6DSOX posture/immobility AI with VCNL4040 IR skin check — blocking dropped-band false alarms while targeting slow DLB/PDD collapses.
  • Resilient ₹65.8/mo Care Circle Escalation: Dual-path MQTTS + direct SMS fallback + T+0/60/180 s multi-tier human claim-lock built for Indian cellular realities.

05 • Industrial & Mechanical Design

What the Band Looks Like: Minimalist Everyday Accessory, Not Hospital Gear

6-Layer Internal Stack-Up (Top to Wrist)

  • Layer 1 — Top Shell & NFC: Matte-graphite polymer shell housing a ferrite-shielded passive NTAG216 NFC inlay.
  • Layer 2 — Skyward Antennas: GNSS ceramic + 4G LTE FPC/LDS antennas on the top outer rim for maximum sky view & SAR safety (≤1.6 W/kg).
  • Layer 3 — 4L/6L Main PCB: Star-routed 3 A cellular power rails, MCU, modem, LSM6DSOX AI IMU, BQ25180 PMIC, MAX17048 gauge, eSIM.
  • Layer 4 — Isolated Battery Bay: 550–700 mAh BIS Li-Po cell separated from modem PA by a 0.5 mm Nomex thermal pad + NTC cutoff.
  • Layer 5 — IP68 Acoustic & O-Ring Seals: Compressed silicone gasket + dual GORE GAW334 ePTFE vents (<2 dB loss, 2 m / 1 hr water block).
  • Layer 6 — Skin Backplate: Flush VCNL4040 IR window, blind-snap 4-pin magnetic pogo charger, permanent laser-etched Emergency QR.
Ergonomics: Breathable perforated silicone avoids Indian summer sweat dermatitis; zero distracting lights or chirps.

20% Emergency Power Reserve & Dead-Zone Safeguards

  • 85% Conservative Usable Capacity: Models real-world 3.55 V cell brownout cutoff under 2–3 A cellular RF bursts.
  • 700 mAh Recommended Standard: Rejects 300 mAh (0 h left after one 10-min call) and 1,000 mAh (too bulky on wrist).
  • Hard 20% SoC Reserve: Automatically halts routine tracking to preserve 29.3 hours of 5-min SOS tracking (or 10-min VoLTE call + 14.7 h tracking).
  • Offline Flash Ring Buffer: Caches ≥40,000 GPS points in dead zones and syncs latest-first (LIFO) upon reconnection.

3-Stage ICMR-Compliant Ethical Validation

  • Golden Rule: Never expose a person living with dementia to unvalidated hardware, simulated wandering, or fall-induction experiments.
  • Stage 1 (Weeks 1–18): Bench RF, RCC indoor tests, Faraday SMS failover, IP67 immersion, and padded-mat fall tests using engineers & healthy young adults only.
  • Stage 2 (Weeks 19–22): 14-day ergonomic & false-positive wear pilot with 15–20 cognitively healthy older adults (60–80 yrs) + family caregivers.
  • Stage 3 (Post-V2 Hospital Pilot): Strictly after Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) approval (ICMR 2017 Sec. 6.8) with capacity evaluation, written consent/LAR + ongoing wearer assent, pictorial 3-point cards, and observational wear only.
